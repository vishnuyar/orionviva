Property tax receipt source guidance:
- paid requires explicit paid evidence. A bill/assessment is bill, not a paid
  receipt. Unknown status remains unknown with the printed supporting page.
- Copy actual payment date, payment/receipt reference, property identity and
  tax year independently. An assessment year is not a payment date.
- source_account_reference is the exact printed escrow/bank source identifier
  when present, not an inferred payer from the property or mortgage lender.
- This receipt corroborates a payment; do not manufacture a second debit or
  an escrow posting. Do not merge different tax years because amounts match.
