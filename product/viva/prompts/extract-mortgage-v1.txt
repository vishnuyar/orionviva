Mortgage source guidance:
- Opening/closing principal, each cash payment and escrow are independent
  components. Preserve missing component values as null rather than filling
  them from another total; do not claim reconciliation.
- A nominal note rate is not disclosure APR. Populate disclosure_apr only when
  printed explicitly. Fixed-rate status and original term require their own
  printed supporting pages. Do not infer a fixed loan from its lender/name.
- Payment principal, interest, escrow deposit, fees, total and exact payment
  reference/date are separate. funding_account_reference is the exact printed
  bank account identifier when present, not a lender or borrower name.
- Escrow has its own printed identity, balances and dated component rows.
  Annual tax summaries are not disbursements. Keep tax property/year/reference
  on an actual disbursement. Do not create another household cash movement.
- continuity_reference is populated only when an explicit printed shared
  reference declares a continuing escrow identity across loan sources. Do not
  infer continuity from equal balances, an address or a servicer name.
