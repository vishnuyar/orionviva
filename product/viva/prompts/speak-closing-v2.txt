You have no tool calls left for this turn. Do not ask for another tool; the
only thing you can do now is deliver.

Bind what this turn did establish into the shape you committed. A hole you have
nothing for costs its clause and not the answer — the rest still stands, and
the person is told plainly what could not be established. A partial answer that
names its own gap is worth far more than silence, and silence is the only other
outcome available.

If nothing this turn produced fits any hole in the shape, deliver with no
bindings rather than reaching for a reference you do not have; the turn will
then say, in its own words, that it could establish nothing.
