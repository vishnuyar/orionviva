Deliver this turn's answer by filling the shape you committed. Call this
exactly once, and only when the tool results from this turn already hold
something for every hole you mean to fill.

You write no words here. The words were settled when you committed the shape;
what is left is to say which thing this turn established fills which hole.

`bindings` maps each hole's name to ONE reference:

```json
{"bindings": {
  "which":   {"entity": "e1"},
  "balance": {"figure": "f3"},
  "when":    {"date": "2026-01-31"},
  "trust":   {"figure": "f3"}
}}
```

The six kinds of reference, and what each must point at:

- `{"figure": "f3"}` — a figure some tool returned in THIS turn. It fills a
  `money`, `count`, `rate` or `grade` hole. A figure that states a currency is
  an amount; one that states none is a plain number; binding one where the
  other belongs is refused. Every figure also says what it measures, in its
  `quantity`, and it must be the same word the hole asked for: a real figure
  about one thing put where the sentence speaks about another is a false
  sentence made of true parts, and it is refused. If nothing this turn measures
  what a hole asked for, leave that hole unbound and lose its clause.
- `{"entity": "e1"}` — something a result spoke about, listed under
  `identifiers`. It fills an `account`, `merchant`, `category` or `document`
  hole, and its own `kind` must be the hole's kind.
- `{"period": "p1"}` — a span from a result's `covers`. It fills a `period`
  hole and nothing else; a period is what a document answers for, never an
  account of what happened inside it.
- `{"date": "2026-01-31"}` — a day some result in this turn carries. A day
  nothing carries cannot be said.
- `{"supposed": "3000"}` — a value the person wrote in their own question,
  exactly as they wrote it.
- `{"read": "r2"}` — a whole read from THIS turn, by the `id` that result
  carries. It fills a `rows` hole and nothing else, and it is how a list is
  said: the machine writes one line per figure that read took over a named
  slice, each line the slice and the figure beside it, with the grade of the
  set once above. Bind the read, never the figures — you do not know how many
  there are, and you are not asked to. A read that took no figure over a named
  slice has no lines in it and refuses.

A hole you leave unbound costs its clause and not the turn: the clause is
dropped and the person is told what could not be established. Bind what you
have; do not reach for a reference you are not sure of, because a reference to
something this turn never produced refuses the whole answer.

Never restate a value, a grade, a date or a name here. All of them travel with
the thing you referred to, and restating one could only get it wrong.

What a result said its own numbers do not cover is placed for you. Every caveat
owed by a figure you bind reaches the person after your answer, verbatim, and
none of it is a reference you write here.
