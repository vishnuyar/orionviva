# tool: query_ledger
Read totals and vocabulary from the ledger. Use entity "balances" for every real account's latest measured balance or amount owed; filter balances by kind "liability" for card/debt totals, which returns both per-account figures and one deterministic total per currency. An asserted account with no measurement is returned as unmeasured and must never be described as zero. Balance rows include their latest evidence date, evidence grade, and evidence limitation. Use entity "transactions" for a filtered summary (count, span, money in, money out, net, and account/month breakdowns), "holdings" for latest measured statement positions, "vocabulary" for known account/category/subcategory/tag/merchant/currency labels, and "aggregate" for a metric.

Aggregate metric "spending" returns spending totals and breakdowns. "income" returns attributed income by source and currency and reports unexplained inflows separately. Income accepts a date window; without one it is explicitly lifetime income over everything ingested. "surplus" directly compares attributed income with counted spending over one shared window; its result has quantity `net_movement`, a positive figure is surplus and a negative figure is shortfall, while unexplained inflows remain separate. "recurring_spending" lists only supported outgoing patterns: a person-confirmed periodicity or a steady monthly/annual rhythm measured by the ledger. Its amounts are historical observations, never forecasts or monthly run rates. "stalest_balance" directly returns the stalest measured account, its evidence date, age in days, current recorded amount, and the honest limitation that the possible financial impact is not bounded. "weakest_evidence" is the direct answer to which balances and transactions have the weakest evidence while being financially significant: it ranks evidence grade first and absolute magnitude second and refuses to compare currencies unless narrowed. "net_worth" is the direct answer to what the person is worth; never assemble net worth yourself from balances and holdings. For a `rows` answer listing net worth by currency, set `view` to `net_by_currency`; that view returns only the net-worth figure for each currency, while the ordinary read retains the assets, liabilities and account audit lines.

A window may use explicit inclusive ISO dates in "from" and "to", or `{"preset":"latest_complete_calendar_month"}`. The preset deterministically resolves to the newest ended whole calendar month shared by posted statement coverage and the result returns the explicit dates it used. Use that preset for "most recent complete calendar month" and reuse the returned explicit dates in follow-up calls. Never substitute lifetime totals for a period question.

Filters narrow by account, category, tag, merchant, currency, or window. Each read refuses filters it does not honor. An account filter accepts an internal account id or a uniquely matching visible account name, ignoring capitalization and repeated spaces; an unknown or ambiguous name is refused, never guessed. Merchant filters take exact keys held by the vault; first use vocabulary with group_by "merchant" and matching when the person gives a name. Use vocabulary directly for "how many" or "which" labels/accounts exist. For an account inventory, combine balances with check_completeness so each item can state its latest evidence date, grade, and gap.

Every monetary value that may be spoken is a figure with an id, quantity, currency, grade, evidence records, and boundary. Bind answer clauses to figure ids; do not copy monetary strings out of data. Liabilities are figures of quantity "owed", not balances held. Never add currencies. Identifiers name the accounts, counterparties, and categories a result discusses; bind names to their ids rather than inventing them. Covers and caveats describe what the result does not establish and must be preserved in the answer.

# tool: list_movements
Read individual matching movement rows. Use this for which purchases, payments, deposits, or tagged movements occurred; query_ledger entity "transactions" is the summary of the same population. Filters are required and must include account, category, merchant, tag, or window; currency alone is too broad. A window accepts explicit ISO edges or the `latest_complete_calendar_month` preset, and the result returns the explicit window used. Each row includes its record id, date, description, effect, currency, nature, category, tags, grade, and source document. Negative effect is money out and positive effect is money in, including on liability accounts. Results are capped and say how many matched.

# tool: check_completeness
Read how complete and current the financial picture is: documents held versus posted, statements awaiting review and why, every account's latest evidence date and grade, and uncategorized counterparties. With `view` set to `attention`, summarize the existing consequence-ordered open-question queue by kind while preserving the queue payload and order. Use it for account inventories, evidence-gap questions, and attention summaries. Do not treat missing or unposted evidence as zero.

# tool: get_provenance
Explain where a figure came from. Give a movement key, account id, or document id as `record_id`. For a classification-treatment explanation, give `movement_phrase` and optional inclusive `from` and `to` dates; matching movements must share one material nature and reason or the read refuses as ambiguous. The result names source evidence, verification grade, and the deterministic decision reason behind a movement's nature. Use this for "why do you say that?" and evidence-strength follow-ups.

# tool: get_transparency
Read the agent's own activity. Topic "agent_activity" lists unattended actions and outcomes; "calls_spent" counts model calls, optionally since a date; "declined_questions" lists questions the person set aside. These are agent-behavior facts, not financial-document facts.

# tool: compute
Perform exact decimal arithmetic over figures already returned this turn. Inputs bind names to figure ids or to `{"stipulated":"<amount the person supplied>"}`. Expressions allow +, -, *, /, parentheses, and integer literals. Never calculate money yourself or type a ledger amount directly into an expression. Money adds/subtracts only in one currency, may multiply/divide by a plain number, and two money figures may divide to a ratio. Invalid units or mixed currencies refuse. The result says whether it is exact, rounded, computed, or hypothetical and carries the evidence rules of its operands.
