Reply with one JSON object whose keys are the slot names above:
{{"<slot name>": <value>}}

How a value is written, by that slot's type:
- money        {{"value": "1234.56", "currency": "USD"}} — the amount in plain
               digits with a dot for the decimal, and the currency they meant.
               Write what they SAID, normalised: "about 250k" is "250000",
               "two fifty" said of a price is "250". Never a figure they did not
               state.
- date         "2019-06-01" — year, month, day.
- rate         "4.25" — a proportion per hundred, digits only.
- yes_no       "yes" or "no".
- choice       exactly one of that slot's listed alternatives, copied
               character for character. Never a word of your own.
- label        their own short word for a thing they named, copied from their
               words rather than tidied into a word you prefer. Many answers
               name no thing at all — leave it empty then, and never manufacture
               a name.
- institution  the public name of the company, as they wrote it.
- link         the account it points at, named exactly as the alternatives are.
- a slot listed as SEVERAL holds a LIST of objects with the parts shown under
  it. Return several where what they described is genuinely several things at
  once, ordered most certain first.

Rules that matter more than completeness:
- Leave a slot out, or empty, rather than guess. An unfilled slot is handled
  properly downstream; a guessed one puts something in someone's finances that
  nobody said.
- NEVER estimate or apportion a proportion. A split nobody stated is left
  empty; that is the correct answer, not a gap.
- Invent no amount, no date, no name. Every value has to be traceable to their
  words or to what is already known above.
- Answer about THIS question only. Anything else they mentioned that no slot
  asks for is dropped.
- What they are describing may sit in any financial instrument, in any country:
  a bank or card account, a brokerage or retirement account, a loan account, a
  wallet. Do not assume a currency, a date order or a jurisdiction.
