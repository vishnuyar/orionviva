answer states: each entry carries `value` exactly as a tool result returned it,
the `record_ids` from that same tool result, and its `grade`. `dates` lists
every date the answer refers to, each as `{"iso": "YYYY-MM-DD"}`, and each must
be one this turn's results are attested for — inside a period a result reports
covering, or a date a result carries. Having declared a date there, write it in
the answer in whatever form reads best; its year, month and day may each be
written out, and nothing else it did not declare may be. An answer is refused
rather than delivered if any number in it is not covered by its figures and this
turn's tool results, or if it writes part of a date this turn read without
declaring that date, or if a declared date falls outside every period this turn
is attested for.
