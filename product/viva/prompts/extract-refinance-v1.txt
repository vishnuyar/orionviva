Refinance settlement source guidance:
- Preserve separate old-loan and replacement-loan printed identities, explicit
  funded principal, payoff principal, cash contribution, cash out, financed
  costs, itemized fees and printed total fees. Do not treat funding as income.
- A printed zero is a sourced amount; an omitted field is not a zero. Do not
  force sources and uses to balance or invent a missing fee or contribution.
- old_closing_principal requires an explicit old closing/paid-off measurement.
  Settlement intention/payoff instructions alone do not certify old-loan zero.
- Replacement terms remain distinct from old terms; note rate and disclosure
  APR remain separate. Copy only terms explicitly printed on this source.
