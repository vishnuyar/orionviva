The closed result schema is `sql-native-income-expense-v1`. It contains period,
committed frontier, limited scope, lines, suppressed bank defaults, per-currency
admitted subtotals and held-source gaps. These are admitted supported-activity
subtotals, not household or net-worth statements. They do not imply unseen
employment/documents, new human classification workflows, investment funding
certification, inferred unknown gains or fresh PDF-field verification.

Dates are exact ISO dates and filter original cash-source dates inclusively. An
empty end resolves to the latest recorded bank transaction, payroll explanation
pay date or investment effect date. The result describes current effective native
state at its returned head, including current transfer/payroll ownership, rather
than reconstructing a historical classification at the period end. Payroll lines
use their actual pay date, while suppression of the linked bank default uses the
bank transaction's own date. Exact native movement ownership connects them even
when the dates differ; equal amount/date descriptions never select a deposit.

Indexed bank/card transaction and posting paths supply provisional source-default
income and expenses. Bank reporting uses the authenticated physical source
account kind and original transaction through the existing pure `money_effect`:
income is the economic movement and expenses its exact sign reversal. A printed
liability charge +10 reports expense +10 while its raw native expense leg stays
-10; a card repayment remains Transfers and is excluded. No absolute value,
refund classification or allocation inversion is introduced. Missing or inconsistent
source kind, transaction, binding or posting ownership refuses the read. Genuine
live transfer membership suppresses those defaults.
An active payroll explanation suppresses its exact net-deposit default and supplies
salary and named tax/insurance/other expense legs once. Its placeholder reversal
is not another income, and retirement bookkeeping is neither an expense nor a new
physical asset. Incompatible active reservations are integrity failures.

Investment selection uses active groups and original immutable effect/leg IDs,
not per-copy posting views or a sum of activation/deactivation history. Whole
copies corroborate without duplicating money; repeated identical source occurrences
remain distinct effects. Rebind adds no money. Inactive effects stay out of the
current report while their reversal/history remains retained. Negative sourced
gains preserve their sign; unknown gains are disclosed rather than invented zero.

Each line retains its exact native posting, actual owning source document,
matching source occurrence or null, and matching investment activity field rows.
Payroll fields and occurrences stay absent where they are absent in the native
source contract; its bank deposit is not payroll-field evidence. Generic posting
grades and classification provisional flags remain distinct from source-field
verification. Related effect IDs name actual native bank/payroll/investment
relationships, not label matches. Returned objects are detached.
