# SQL payroll explanations

This describes the prototype-v4 SQL payroll family, whose bounded focused owner
passed fifty controls before the investment preparation changed shared owners.
The later combined investment candidate passed 303 focused controls with
independent terminal review before subsequent shared-source accounting edits.
These are historical bounded results, not a current package/full or corpus pass. Production application sessions still
use their event-log authority. The earlier complete-verification foundation
passes are historical; the separate application-storage candidate stopped after
188 passes and 34 failures among 222 cases. Payroll work does not qualify or
retry that storage repair. Brokerage, retirement and instrument-family completion,
all-794 correctness and timing, hosted acceptance and installed delivery remain open.

A payroll document explains an already accepted depository inflow. Its gross
minus deductions must equal net exactly. A unique unlinked deposit must have
the same currency and net amount and lie within ten days of pay date. Missing
deposits wait; multiple deposits or different meanings competing for one deposit
are ambiguous. An equal amount alone never chooses a winner.

The explanation books salary minus gross, reverses the deposit's uncategorized
income placeholder by plus net, and books each deduction into its existing
universal bucket. These additional legs sum to zero and never change bank cash
or create another bank transaction. Salary retains the existing pure arithmetic
posting's verified grade; categories and deductions remain unverified. A
retirement deduction is not proof of a destination holding or independently
verified funding. Deduction page provenance remains unavailable when the typed
facts do not supply it.

Eight dedicated SQL tables retain typed financial facts, current components,
indexed dependencies, immutable explanations, corroborating document witnesses,
variable-count immutable legs, activation decisions and the latest active view.
All participate in authenticated audit replay and complete verification. Bank
transactions, two-leg bank postings and account balances keep their existing
meaning. Distinct source encodings with the same exact economic signature share
one effective explanation. Signatures normalize employer/employee whitespace
and case, preserve employee multiplicity and deduction order/multiplicity, and
normalize only decimal representation. Every retained original and receipt stays
separate. Global effective legs are returned once, retaining their first source's
provenance.

Indexed closure runs inside the actual causal writer before sealing its audit
and committing. Payroll admission, a newly admitted bank deposit and relevant
manual transfer membership changes reconsider affected matching claims. Later
ambiguity or linkage retires the effective explanation; later resolution can
reactivate it. Immutable legs and witness history are retained. Successor decision
ordinals record the actual writer revision and the exact authenticated input
operations that caused activation or retirement. Unchanged state creates no new
financial decision. There is no new transfer confirmation policy or backdating.

Complete verification independently interprets retained payroll receipts, checks
source ownership where applicable, reconstructs actual bank candidates and claim
ambiguity at every authenticated audit prefix, recomputes balanced pure posting
legs and checks the entire activation/deactivation history. Agreement with audited
rows alone is insufficient: a consistently audited wrong income amount, missing
leg/witness or false causal boundary must still be refused. Ordinary settlement
uses indexed SQL and never rebuilds a Ledger projection or scans all payroll for
an unrelated capture. Full prefix reconstruction is explicit verification work
required by the owner's complete-every-decision policy.

Direct `SQLAuthority` exposes `payroll_facts(doc_id)`,
`payroll_component(doc_id)`, `payroll_decisions(doc_id)` and
`payroll_postings(doc_id=None)`. These are held canonical read decisions with
detached return values. A document-specific posting read resolves a current
accepted explanation, including a duplicate witness; the no-argument read returns
each active explanation once. Application storage's closed forwarding list is
unchanged, so these getters are not yet exposed through the application adapter.

Typed PayStubFacts and exact Decimal/Deduction types are checked before financial
acceptance. Retained payroll source extensions preserve their explicit v1 or v2
wire format. Genuine issued curated admissions also authenticate the original,
member and retained envelope, use the existing payroll parser and validate
available page bounds. The current curated payroll envelopes have no source
cells or event identifiers. Typed facts, retained source and arithmetic do not
establish independent PDF field agreement or public financial acceptance; missing
field channels remain missing.

The payroll-only candidate introduced prototype-v4/schema4 and inner engine-v2.
The later investment Step A source preparation introduces fresh prototype-v5,
schema5 and inner engine-v3, with no automatic migration or adoption. Keyed sealed
history retains the exact historical pairs (v3,3), (v4,4) and the new (v5,5),
rejecting boolean/string versions. Accepting historical sealed genesis does not
open an old database. Keyless history checks the actual sealed chain structure;
it does not decrypt the database or certify financial values or source fields.

The original meaningful unsupported-payroll reproduction and all fifty payroll
assertions remain retained. Their earlier focused result is historical evidence
for the payroll candidate, not a current combined-source pass. No support for all
sixty payroll documents, production activation, performance improvement or main
integration follows from that bounded result.
