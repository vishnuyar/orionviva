# ADR-017 — Apply accounting interpretations and learn from corrections

**Status:** Accepted by the owner, 2026-10-04, with correction and default-purpose amendments on 2026-10-05; implemented and independently verified with findings. Complete acceptance, actual-document Witness and signed release readiness remain open.

## Decision

Replace proactive classification review with automatic, reversible accounting interpretation. Valid transactions contribute to working balance-sheet and profit-and-loss reports immediately. Models determine meaning from a bounded categorical envelope; deterministic code owns all amounts and report arithmetic. Human explanations apply without a redundant confirmation and establish scoped learning. Source documents, account kinds, supported links and explicit allocations remain stronger than guesses.

Original events remain immutable. Separate accounting control and restoration events retain correction history and allow undo. Classification evidence cannot strengthen measurement evidence. Unknown debt components or missing balances remain visible without generating classification questions.

## Consequences

The owner amended the interpretation contract on 2026-10-05: “you do not need evidence for category and treatment, evidence is required only for source of numbers”. Accounting prompt v3 describes only the counterpart of an already recorded source posting. Paying through a liability source does not itself add a liability counterpart. Categories and compound meaning remain eligible for provisional inference; no categorical proof gate is introduced. Component amounts and arithmetic remain local and require numeric allocation evidence.

The manifest names v1/v2 as older contracts whose automatic-model, unverified, multiple-leg, empty-share treatments retire at the next interpretation entry. Restoration appends before a reread and refreshes the effective projection. It preserves stronger treatments and valid single asset/liability interpretations; v3 compounds survive unchanged runs. An unavailable or unsuccessful reread leaves the ordinary baseline usable, while a valid inferred compound can again remain unresolved numerically. Reload alone is not a migration.

The spending chart admits default and inferred expense meaning subject to its source-number, identity, coverage, currency and overlap safeguards. Persona pack v51 introduced inferred non-spending treatment and missing component amounts without demanding classification confirmation; the current pack-v52 distinguishes inferred and human compound wording. That automatic counterpart amendment added no outbound fields or destinations; intentionally revised instruction bytes are versioned and old prompt/persona bytes stay immutable. Actual-model compliance is reserved for the paid Witness restart; synthetic dispatch and restoration tests cannot establish it.

M1 permits automatically inferred asset/liability treatment with provenance and reversibility. X3 reserves confirmation for irreversible operations and unresolved targets, not reversible interpretation. Previous question-first documents remain historical descriptions of their requested-editor substrate; the active behavior is specified in [automatic-accounting.md](../automatic-accounting.md).

No private transaction descriptors, amounts or account identifiers enter the automatic classification envelope. Only shipped public taxonomy labels and bounded sanitized examples may be transmitted. Current extraction adapters lack search tools; broader public research is an explicitly unsupported capability rather than a hidden assumption.

The cost is more responsibility for inference attribution, scoped matching, correction receipts, source-link navigation and exact canonical/SQL parity. Uncertainty becomes visible coverage and provisional contribution rather than user homework.

## Dedicated correction contract amendment

The owner approved the bounded selected accounting-correction repair on 2026-10-05. The correction model now receives source role and account-kind-aware direction in addition to the explicitly submitted explanation and selected count. Selection remains local. Homogeneous selections use scalar fields; mixed selections use aligned role/direction lists in selection order, never the first member's context for all members. A shared interpretation must fit the entire selection; different required counterparts can yield an unsuccessful correction rather than a partial application. This is truthful context, not a guarantee that all mixed selections share one meaning.

These categorical outbound additions and the immutable dedicated `accounting-correct-v1` instructions are the explicit privacy-boundary change. No selected source descriptions, account identifiers, names, dates, currencies, amounts or excerpts are added; no destination or provider changes. General `interpret-v4`, automatic `accounting-interpret-v3` and all older released bytes remain immutable and retain their separate contracts.

Each correction leg describes only a counterpart of the already posted source. A temporary `counterpart_mode` distinguishes ordinary generic meaning from genuine component/holding treatment. Explicit ordinary mode requires one major with empty hint and share, resolves to the existing generic bucket and opens no holding. Card-source repayment uses an asset counterpart (transfer); bank-source repayment uses a liability counterpart (settlement). An invalid or inconsistent ordinary response fails before financial application without a retry or prefix. Components and missing-mode legacy readings retain identity safeguards; unnamed holdings still need a name and ambiguous targets still need identification. Genuine same-role and asset-plus-liability compounds remain inferable without categorical proof, with unstated allocations unresolved.

The mode is consumed locally before persistence. Ruling, correction and learned-rule schemas are unchanged; raw response capture may retain the mode. Learning keeps the existing narrow party/privacy/direction/source-role/purpose context and does not broaden history. Undo remains append-only. Existing human corrections, created accounts and learned rules are not automatically retired. Source-number evidence remains the owner's boundary: categorical meaning may be inferred, but amounts, balances and allocations cannot be manufactured.

The combined correction/default-purpose implementation has fresh independent synthetic verification, recorded in [automatic-accounting.md](../automatic-accounting.md). Complete acceptance and paid browser Witness acceptance remain incomplete. Provider-free regression checks establish mechanics only. The initial automatic unclassified-income miss and automatic v3's general repayment wording remain outside this amendment.

## Default-purpose learning amendment

The owner approved a bounded exception to exact learned-purpose matching on 2026-10-05. A row with effective category `other` and subcategory `unclassified`, both independently default-attributed, may receive an otherwise matching lesson. Missing records or labels are ineligible. Specific category and subcategory attribution takes precedence over record-wide legacy attribution. Human, model and merchant assignments remain known context even when they name the fallback labels; known purpose keeps exact matching and latest-eligible-rule precedence.

For a default-unknown row, all otherwise eligible exact and compatible rules form one pool. Different semantic counterpart legs or output categories cause refusal of learned treatment, leaving ordinary automatic interpretation available. Equivalent outputs use the latest eligible rule; component order does not create a different meaning. This inference may fill an unknown purpose, but cannot overrule a known conflicting purpose or identify a destination bank. It creates no classification question, proof requirement or extra paid retry.

The compatibility policy applies to existing and new saved rules at the next normal interpretation entry. Recorded contexts, rule bodies, dates, recurrence and excluded movements remain unchanged; reload alone does not apply a lesson. Party, privacy, direction, source role and private source-account fences, explicit correction precedence and append-only restoration remain intact. No schema, prompt or outbound envelope changes. Source amounts, balances, dates, currency and allocations remain immutable. Broadening earlier lessons' eligibility is an explicitly approved semantic consequence, not a rule migration. Independent verification and actual-document Witness evidence are required for this amendment.
