## Why this doc

Filenames carry no numbers, by decision (2026-07-19). Numbered filenames make order structural: inserting a document between two others forces renames or, worse, discourages the insertion. Order is editorial, so it lives here and only here. A new document gets written under a plain name and slotted into this guide — nothing else moves.

A pay stub **decomposes a deposit** (its net corroborates the checking deposit — Slice 3 reuse — while the withheld tax/retirement/insurance become their own legs), income is recognized once, and deductions sort into universal buckets (I5, no US-shaped table). Slice 4; establishes that "income is not one shape" (a 1099 is a sibling profile, not a subtype) and seeds the employer-Party and recurring-Obligation threads.
- [merchantcore-package.md](merchantcore-package.md) — the **merchant enrichment package**, a peer to vivacore that the product consumes. `vivacore` is the trust/verification core; `merchantcore` is the merchant knowledge base (normalize · MerchantRecord · Enricher · Catalog · commons). Deep design of the three flows: the product submits **only impersonal** merchant hints (T5 at a package boundary — no amounts/dates/accounts ever cross), merchantcore makes its **own batched model calls** to enrich merchants into multi-attribute records, and the product **syncs results back as events** so its ledger stays self-contained (T4). Slice 5.6; the home for the enrichment prompt, multi-attribute merchants (website/socials/reviews later), and the commons registry. **Superseded in part 2026-07-28** — read the next entry with it.
- [the-conduit-and-the-counterparty.md](the-conduit-and-the-counterparty.md) — **built 2026-07-28**: where the descriptor ends and the grammar begins. A rail is a conduit and the party at the other end is the counterparty, so what a normalizer may keep is decided structurally rather than by a word list; `linted_example` runs inside the catalog rather than at its callers, so the privacy lint is a property of the store; and `word_owners` is fenced as a diagnostic. This is the document that closed the cold read's C2.
- [merchant-catalog-and-commons.md](merchant-catalog-and-commons.md) — **categorize the merchant, not the transaction**: a normalized merchant → category **catalog** (a prior; your per-transaction ruling overrides it) fed by a *batched* model call over new merchants only (O(new-merchants), not O(transactions)), applied retrospectively. The load-bearing privacy split: the raw descriptor (with order-ids and peer names) stays **encrypted**; only a linted, impersonal **commercial merchant→category catalog** is ever unencrypted or shared — which makes it the seed of a content-addressed **categorization commons** (your ruling spares the next user). Deterministic + versioned normalization (never fuzzy-matching), locale-sharded. Slice 5.5; the first real network effect, and what makes categorization usable at scale.
- [categories-and-tags.md](categories-and-tags.md) — **one partitions, one overlays.** A **category** is exactly one per movement, so the parts sum to the whole and "where did my money go?" is *checkable*; a **tag** is many per movement, overlapping, and tag totals **deliberately do not sum**. Mixing them yields a report whose parts do not add up to its total, which in this product is a bluff — so `spending_by_tag` returns `untagged` and `total` beside the per-tag figures and callers must show them. The question that opened it also **explained the category sprawl** rather than sitting beside it: `poker`, `martial arts`, `down payment` are tags that were typed into a category field because a category field was the only one there was. And the split *is* T9 at the label level — a category is shareable world knowledge (a merchant IS a coffee shop, for everyone), a tag is personal meaning (this coffee was on the Japan trip) that no commons can know, which is why tags get their own event type and "tags never leave this device" is an event-level rule. Slice 7.6.
- [categorization-and-spending.md](categorization-and-spending.md) — makes "where did my money go?" answerable. First job (a Slice-4 real-data finding): the **kind-aware counter-leg** (a card purchase is spending, a card payment a debt reduction — fixing the sign-inverted Uncategorized buckets). Then a **category as a graded overlay** via correction-as-event (model suggests `unverified`, you confirm `verified`), a minimal jurisdiction-neutral seed taxonomy (I5), and a real spending-by-category projection composing with transfer-exclusion (S3). The one seam Core leaves open: every categorization **captures the merchant descriptor**, so merchant normalization + a shareable **merchant→category commons** (a lazy, locale-sharded *prior*, [format-commons](format-commons.md)-style) and learned auto-apply become a later *projection over events already recorded* — nothing wasted, no re-ingest. Slice 5 (core); single-category + suggest-and-confirm, with splits/tags/merchant-learning deferred.
- [honest-aggregates-and-the-learning-loop.md](honest-aggregates-and-the-learning-loop.md) — what the first full real-vault run taught: "spending" was computed as *money that left an account*, when it must mean **money that left your life** (M1). Two systems described the same fact — a transfer **link** (excluded) and a *category* saying "transfers" (still counted) — and the aggregate listened to only one; worse, one category covered two opposite natures (a mortgage payment vs a payment to your own card). The fix is a derived **movement nature** — linked → counterparty is your own account → a human ruling → category as a mere *suggestion* → default spending — with anything undecided reported as **provisional** rather than silently resolved. It also names the bigger shape: the four ask-and-learn loops already built (identity, transfer, category, merchant) are one primitive, and the **question queue** is the learning loop's front door — ranked by how much money answering it moves. The standing rule it establishes: *abstract the read side early (cheap, reversible), the write side late (expensive, one-way)*. Slice 6.5.
- [the-question-queue.md](the-question-queue.md) — the learning loop's **front door**, and the sequel to honest aggregates. The project had built four ask-and-learn loops separately (whose account? · same money? · what merchant? · spending or moving?) — four queues, four cards, one primitive. This unifies them as a *read-side* `Question` projection ranked by **consequence**: ask the question that moves the most money first, scope it to the most general unit that is still honest (one ruling should clear many), and summarize the tail rather than hiding it. Question text is a deterministic template, never a model call — a model that phrases a question could smuggle a claim into it. Answers route to the writers that already exist, so the slice adds no event type; the generic `Ruling` (and category-scoped rules) waits for Move 3. Where the persona work (uncertainty language, when-to-speak) finally gets a concrete surface. Slice 6.5 Move 2.
- [the-surface-cards.md](the-surface-cards.md) — **Slice 6.9, spec 2026-07-26, BUILT: one card per instrument KIND, and no build step.** Reverses 6.7's React choice on evidence: a compiled bundle can serve last hour's product with no error and no way to tell by looking — the same stale-artifact failure the project spent a week finding elsewhere, in a repo whose discipline is *the artifact must not lie*. Reversing a two-way door when the evidence changes is the process working. Settles what each card shows: a depository leads with its balance AND its as-of date; a liability speaks *owed*, the figure on the bill, and calls out a credit balance; an investment shows the statement's own `cash + Σ holdings = total` identity, because showing the cross-check *is* the trust, and names activity it could NOT post; an asserted asset says **cost**, badged as your word, with the document that would corroborate it. Every card carries the same three things — the figure, its as-of date and grade, and what it does not include.
- [the-presentation-layer.md](the-presentation-layer.md) — the surface catches up. The engine had outrun it *measurably*: four endpoints the page never called (two of them since Slice 5) and seven overview fields it ignored, including all of the holdings, the honesty signals, and a held document whose invisibility had just been fixed in the data. Rebuilds the page around the **question queue as its spine** (the picture → what Viva needs → your money → where it went), with one-tap answers inline and a focused detail view for anything needing context. Records the four rulings: hybrid answering; categories stay **implicit** (suggestions + ones you've used + add-your-own, no new event) with the named-but-unused wrinkle accepted; peer descriptors finally get per-transaction categorization; and **React + Vite** — chosen over a zero-dependency file split for legibility to readers and reliability of AI-written code, bounded by static output, no runtime CDN, and zero new dependencies in the trust path. Slice 6.7.
- [prompts-as-files.md](prompts-as-files.md) — **why every prompt is a `.txt` file and none is a Python literal.** The discipline existed in one module and drifted twice anyway, because the library *was code*: adding a prompt meant editing a module while a local triple-quoted string was one line, and friction beats intent. Records a real T8 failure found in the audit (`enrich-v2`'s text existed only in git history while events named it), the recovery, and the two tests that make recurrence a build failure rather than a hope.
- [where-the-intelligence-goes.md](where-the-intelligence-goes.md) — **read this BEFORE the 9a spec below.** Written after running 9a on a real vault: the machinery works and is aimed at the wrong moment. It made the *answer* intelligent and left the *question* naive — asking "is this spent or owned?" about a counterparty already enriched as `loan_payments/mortgage`. Argues the inverse: **the product forms the belief, the person confirms it.** Introduces the missing idea — a merchant category *implies structure* (a mortgage servicer implies a property, a loan, escrow, a 1098) — and places that knowledge in merchantcore at enrichment time, where it is impersonal, batched, cached and commons-shareable, rather than in a per-sentence personal call. Sorts every movement into three tiers (silence / informed proposal / real question), reuses the forced-suggested-unlocalized ladder, and owns the drift that prompted it: five keyword tables written in one slice, against a stated anti-goal.
- [from-your-words-to-the-ledger.md](from-your-words-to-the-ledger.md) — **Slice 9a, built 2026-07-25**: how a sentence becomes double-entry. Diagnoses why the three-button question failed on real data — `nature` was an impoverished stand-in for *what the counter-leg is*, when the ledger already speaks double-entry's own vocabulary internally. Settles the ontology: **four majors** (expense / asset / liability / income), equity deliberately absent because for a person it *is* net worth and is derived; fixed top, free hierarchy below. Six-step toolset with **exactly one model call** — and the step that resolves "which account is this?" turns out to be Slice 1.5's matcher pointed at a new target, so it inherits ask-only-when-ambiguous and learn-the-ruling for free. Carries the three cases that shaped it: a car (records **cost**, not value), a mortgage (three legs with **proportions honestly unknown** → ask for the statement), a car loan (**balance unknown** and said so). Ends with *What changed in the building* — a fifth nature (`mixed`), two rung bugs the tests caught, and two things the spec got wrong about the substrate.
- [viva-listens-and-speaks.md](viva-listens-and-speaks.md) — **are the agent and the learning loop one thing or two?** One engine, two directions: you *say what something is* (write) or *ask what is true* (read). They differ in what can go wrong — a bad ruling persists and generalizes, a bad answer misleads once — so Slice 9 splits, and **listening comes first** (it needs no new tools and de-risks the hardest safety property in a bounded setting). Research (July 2026) backs the boundary rather than softening it: intent-to-execution integrity, structured output over open text, and human confirmation as *state of the art* rather than training wheels. The finding that changed a decision: agent-memory frameworks are built for *preferences*, while our rulings are **institutional knowledge** — so their hard problems (staleness, trustworthy retrieval, when memory should stay silent) are ones our event log avoids by construction. **Do not adopt a memory framework.** Centrepiece is the growth architecture: one new block, **Proposal** — the write-side twin of Finding — which turns transfer suggestions, model category guesses, forced corrections, drafted budgets and eventual actions into instances of one thing, and makes X3 (nothing irreversible without an explicit yes) a property of the type rather than a rule to remember.
- [viva-persona.md](viva-persona.md) — **who Viva is**: the butler persona in full — identity, traits, guiding principles, the conversational arc, the question library mapped to the machinery behind each kind, the "I don't know" handling, and the first-session script. The definition every pack phrasing is written under (and the copywriter model drafts against); what the persona must never do is part of the definition, not an appendix.
- [viva-persona-and-interview.md](viva-persona-and-interview.md) — **Viva decides HOW to ask, never WHAT**: the persona as a rendering-and-interviewing layer over the queue, and the persona guide mapped to its real destinations — voice phrasings, attribute schemas, expectation entries — all *data packs*, the constitution applied to the butler himself. Four decisions (2026-07-27): the persona is Viva as [viva-persona.md](viva-persona.md) defines her; **phrasebook now, live phrasing only when earned** (every sentence Viva can say is reviewable before she says it; a template's failure mode is stiff, never false); cards + one-question-at-a-time with "not now" as a remembered answer (the **decline event** — settled → silence, applied to questions); sequence voice → expectations → asset interview (P1–P3, proposed 6.10–6.12). Attributes land `asserted` and documents upgrade them — the net-worth D3 pattern become the profile's whole grammar.
- [the-interview-and-the-schema-pack.md](the-interview-and-the-schema-pack.md) — **the schema owns what may be asked; the model owns what to ask next; the person owns whether anything is created.** Supersedes and absorbs P3 above. A question with a next step, built read-side: no interview object and no interview event, only a projection over the attribute rulings and declines a subject already carries. The **schema pack** is the fourth pack — shape as data, jurisdiction-tagged with `us` and `in` from the first entry, `essential` ranking and terminating and gating net worth, `unlocks` giving every question a reason, `opens` expressing branching as data rather than as a loop. Nine decisions (2026-08-01), of which the load-bearing ones are D1 (read-side, no new event type), D2 (a closed vocabulary a model may select within), D6 (essentials terminate the interview and an unfilled one is a **disclosed gap**, never a zero) and D8 (cycle 1 is deterministic; the model selector must beat it on a measured number). Governed by [ADR-012](decisions/ADR-012-the-interview-model-boundary.md). Cycle 1 built 2026-08-01; how a schema finds an account is recorded in the spec's own as-built note.
- [document-coverage.md](document-coverage.md) — **every instrument a financial life produces, and which ones the product has actually eaten.** Five types are covered; a real life produces closer to forty. Doubles as the corpus plan and the profile/prompt backlog: each row becomes a registry row + prompt fragment + identity, most of them data rather than code. Ordered by what each gap *blocks* — the mortgage statement is top because without it a question we already ask has no correct answer. Includes the India column deliberately (I5), because a taxonomy that only fits the US isn't a taxonomy.
- [learning-mode.md](learning-mode.md) — **superseded 2026-07-25 by [from-your-words-to-the-ledger.md](from-your-words-to-the-ledger.md); read it for the diagnosis.** The two questions the queue asked and shouldn't have, and what they reveal. A **mortgage payment is three things at once** (interest spent, principal buying equity, escrow still yours), so no single spending/moved/own answer is correct — the honest move is not to make the person guess but to *ask for the document that states the split* ("documents are evidence that other documents exist"), since the split mechanism (`split_transaction`) has existed unused since v0 and only the ratios are missing. A **car purchase** needs the Asset primitive — enumerated, unbuilt — where a vehicle's value is `estimated`, never `measured`, which is what the valuation-class discipline was for. Also records Vishnu's postulate: **rulings in your own words**, with the boundary that keeps it safe — a model parses *intent* into a structured proposal, never a figure; you confirm; deterministic code applies. Deferred by decision, documented so the questions stay asked.
- [local-categorization-and-custom-categories.md](local-categorization-and-custom-categories.md) — the categorization thread's private half, surfaced by the first real enrichment run: peer descriptors (Zelle/Venmo) have no stable merchant category — one is a gift, another a loan repayment — so they need a **per-transaction, local** path the commons can't and shouldn't touch (T9). Captures four decisions for the presentation layer: peer descriptors default to per-transaction not "everywhere"; **custom categories are first-class but strictly local** (a user's "Gifts"/"Loan to Raj" never reach merchantcore or the commons); and a movement's **nature** (spending vs transfer/settlement) so peer money a user marks "loan repaid" stays out of spending. The substrate already supports it (`CategoryAssigned` is movement-keyed and overrides the merchant prior); the note settles the model before the UI is built.
- [net-worth.md](net-worth.md) — **Slice 7, spec settled 2026-07-26**: net worth is **a curve, not a number with a date attached**. `net_worth(D)` is defined at every date between your earliest document and your latest, each point built from every account's last-known measurement at or before D — which dissolves the as-of problem rather than answering it, needs no new event type, and yields trends for free. Settles four decisions: **trust the user** (the provable/unprovable line is an *audience* question, so the disclosure view is later a one-line filter, not a subsystem); **two kinds of unknown** (an *asserted* value is trusted and badged, an *undecomposable* one is asked for, recorded, and corrected when the document arrives); **reuse the grade ladder** ("provable" *is* `corroborated` — a parallel issued/asserted badge would be two systems describing one fact, the bug that inflated spending in 6.5); and **per-currency subtotals, never conversion**, because a converted figure is one no document attests. Every point names its stalest input, so the curve visibly firms up as documents arrive.
- [positions-and-investments.md](positions-and-investments.md) — the first **asset that isn't cash**: a brokerage/retirement statement's holdings. The load-bearing decision is that a holding is a **dated measurement, not a posting** (a `PositionObserved` event measured at the statement date, like a balance observation) — so the money ledger stays pure cash flow and **unrealized gain is the difference between measurements, never a fabricated transaction** (the thesis: measurements, not generations). Introduces the `INVESTMENT` account kind, a snapshot reconciliation identity `Σ(market_value) + cash = total` (the densest, most model-free cross-check yet), and the **valuation-class invariant** — a `measured` value is always shown with its as-of date, never dressed as "current" (the "never bluff a number" wall applied to stale prices, inherited by every future asset). The brokerage account is also a **reconciliation hub**: a cross-account cash flow (`opening + Σ activity = closing`) ties contributions to the funding checking/savings account via Slice-3 links (counted once), recognizes dividends/interest as income and fees as expense, and books a sell's realized gain — all *realized cash*, while unrealized change stays a presentation view (M1). Slice 6 (both stages built); a divergent profile as *data* (Slice-4 reuse), cost basis seeding Tax (S11), positions seeding net worth (S7).
- [local-first-storage-and-crypto.md](local-first-storage-and-crypto.md) — where truth lives: encrypted storage, key custody, tamper-evidence.
- [threat-model-and-ingestion-security.md](threat-model-and-ingestion-security.md) — adversaries by ruin-vs-bad-day; document prompt injection and why the extraction model is powerless by design (the CaMeL pattern, arrived at independently). Also the home of the supply-chain argument for hand-written HTTP model adapters — no provider SDK on the path between a bank statement and the wire.
- [own-chain-vs-borrowed-trust.md](own-chain-vs-borrowed-trust.md) — why we anchor to existing fortresses instead of building a chain: the ION and Sovrin precedents, the node-churn physics, and where "every app is a node" honestly fits (verification, not storage).

## Placement rules

New documents are slotted here at writing time, wherever they belong conceptually — that's the point of unnumbered names. Documents whose *content* was replaced stay in the folder, marked **Superseded** in their status line with a pointer forward. Documents that were true of a *closed phase* — a plan for work now done, a snapshot of a market on a date — get the ⛔ **HISTORICAL RECORD** banner and move into [archived/](archived/README.md), with their entry recorded in section 6 above. The distinction matters: superseded means *we changed our minds*, historical means *this was true then*. Neither is ever deleted; a project arguing that trust must be provable does not quietly erase its own reasoning — it fences it off where no reader can mistake it for the present. If this guide and a doc's own cross-references ever disagree about structure, this guide wins.
