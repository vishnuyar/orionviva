# Financial picture

## PICTURE-001 — Sample picture remains stable

**Actions:** Open the sample experience, wait for it to settle, navigate away,
and return to Overview.

**Pass:** Expected sample figures remain visible after settling and navigation;
the page does not briefly show figures and then become empty.

## PICTURE-002 — Overview explains the current picture

**Prerequisite:** A vault contains imported synthetic or private documents.

**Actions:** Open Overview and inspect its main figures, labels, and coverage.

**Pass:** The page shows a coherent picture or an explicit limitation. It does
not present missing coverage as a complete financial truth.

## PICTURE-003 — Accounts reconcile with their source coverage

**Actions:** Open Accounts and compare each visible account with the documents
the product says support it.

**Pass:** Accounts are not silently merged or duplicated, and balances are
either supported by evidence or clearly marked uncertain/incomplete.

## PICTURE-004 — Transactions remain navigable

**Actions:** Open Transactions, use available filters or grouping, inspect an
item, navigate away, and return.

**Pass:** The list remains populated, controls give understandable results, and
the selected period or filter does not unexpectedly reset.

## PICTURE-005 — Statements account for every imported document

**Actions:** Open Statements after import completion.

**Pass:** Every selected document has a durable outcome, and opening a document
or its status does not close the vault or empty the page.

## PICTURE-006 — Current-period ranges stay visible

**Actions:** Select each available current-period range, wait for the page to
settle, and navigate between financial surfaces.

**Pass:** The selected range is understandable and its results remain visible.
An empty result is explicitly described rather than rendered as a blank page.

## PICTURE-007 — Cross-surface figures are consistent

**Actions:** Follow the same balance, net-worth value, or transaction grouping
from Overview to its detailed surface and evidence.

**Pass:** Figures agree at the same scope and period. Any intentional difference
is explained in plain language, including what was included or excluded.

## PICTURE-008 — Unknown coverage is not guessed

**Actions:** Inspect a period or account for which the test set is intentionally
incomplete.

**Pass:** The product marks the gap, uncertainty, or unavailable answer and does
not fabricate a zero, institution, classification, or complete-history claim.
