# Review, plans, and Ask Viva

## GUIDE-001 — Review queue explains the decision

**Actions:** Open Review with at least one synthetic item needing attention.

**Pass:** The user can understand what needs a decision, what evidence exists,
and the effect of each available action.

## GUIDE-002 — Review decisions update all affected surfaces

**Actions:** Resolve one review item and revisit related totals, transactions,
and evidence.

**Pass:** The resolution is durable and consistent; the item does not silently
reappear or cause unrelated values to change.

## GUIDE-003 — Create and revisit a plan

**Actions:** Create a supported plan from synthetic data, leave the surface,
restart the application, and return.

**Pass:** The plan remains available with its target, progress, assumptions,
and relationship to the underlying financial picture intact.

## GUIDE-004 — Ask Viva answers an answerable question with evidence

**Actions:** Ask a plain-language question that the current vault can answer.

**Pass:** The answer directly addresses the question, uses the correct scope,
and exposes supporting evidence or provenance. Arithmetic agrees with the
deterministic financial surfaces.

## GUIDE-005 — Ask Viva refuses an unanswerable question honestly

**Actions:** Ask a question requiring information absent from the vault.

**Pass:** The answer names the missing information or limitation and does not
invent a number, source, certainty, or completed action.

## GUIDE-006 — Conversation context is durable and bounded

**Actions:** Ask a follow-up that clearly depends on the prior question, then
leave and return to the conversation through the supported flow.

**Pass:** Supported context is retained without mixing another vault's data.
If persistence is not supported for the flow, the limitation is explicit.
