## Independent calculation conventions

Money inputs are finite nonnegative decimals, at most 15 integer and six fractional digits. Rates are nominal annual percentages from zero to 100; horizons are one to 600 months. Normalize each monetary input once to cents using HALF_EVEN; round monthly interest and endpoints to cents. Nominal annual rate is divided by twelve, without daily accrual, prorating, fees, tax, inflation or business-day adjustment.

Savings and cash start their first monthly endpoint one month after the opening date. Loan first-payment date is the first modeled payment after one full monthly interest period. Preserve the original day and clamp each target month independently: January 31 becomes February 28/29 then March 31. Check calendar overflow refusals.

Savings final balance and contributed principal are hypothetical stocks at the final date; growth is a net movement over the modeled period. Loan remaining debt is owed stock; applied repayments and explicitly labelled interest component are repayment flows. Count only positive applied payments. At a positive rate, payment must exceed initial modeled monthly interest. At zero rate a genuine zero payment leaves positive debt unchanged, with no payoff date; a positive payment rounding to zero while modeled debt is positive refuses. Starting zero has no invented positive payments or payoff date.

Cash reports final balance, minimum monthly endpoint and first-negative endpoint/date when present. These do not prove within-month liquidity or affordability.
